Privacy Policy — Country Quartet

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Country Quartet is published by Mindgrid Games.

The short version

Country Quartet does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no internet access at all.

What the app stores

The app stores two things, and both stay on your device:

  • Settings — whether animations are switched on.
  • Statistics — games played, games won, games lost, draws, quartets collected and best score.

These are written to the app's own private storage on your device using Android's DataStore. They are never uploaded anywhere, because the app has no network permission and no server to upload them to.

You can clear the statistics at any time with Reset Statistics in the app's Settings screen. Uninstalling the app removes everything it has stored.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request the INTERNET permission, or any other permission.
  • It does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location or device identifiers.
  • It contains no advertising.
  • It contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It contains no in-app purchases.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in or user-generated content.

Children

The app is designed for children as well as adults. Because it collects no data of any kind, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be described here.
